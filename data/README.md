# Data

## Included (`processed/`)

Small district-level outputs, enough to reproduce the factor analysis tables, the consumption-quintile result and the fifty-year comparison without the raw panels.

| File | Rows | What it holds | Built in |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `shock_matrix.parquet` | 38 years × 214 districts | Detrended kharif output shocks used for the correlation and PCA analysis | NB5 |
| `factor_loadings.parquet` | 214 districts | Varimax loadings on the six risk regions (F1–F6) and region assignment | NB5 |
| `systematic_share.parquet` | 214 districts | Beta to the common factor, systematic share of shock variance, structural and SHRUG covariates | NB5 |
| `h1_era_exposure.parquet` | 248 districts | Drought exposure estimated separately for 1966–90 and 1991–2015 | NB2 |

## Not included (raw inputs)

The notebooks expect these files in `/content/drive/My Drive/Data/Elevar` (the `BASE` path at the top of each notebook). They are public but large, and some carry their own redistribution terms, so they are not committed.

**ICRISAT District Level Database** (apportioned to 1966 district boundaries). Main crop panel (`ICRISAT-District Level Data.csv`), district GDP (`ICRISAT-District Level Data Gdp.csv`), and the modules saved as `icrisat_rainfall_monthly.csv`, `icrisat_rainfall_normal.csv`, `icrisat_landuse.csv`, `icrisat_irr_sourcewise.csv`, `icrisat_census_population.csv`, `icrisat_census_holdings.csv`, `icrisat_banks.csv`, `icrisat_markets_roads.csv`, `icrisat_milk.csv`, `icrisat_fertilizer.csv`, `icrisat_soil.csv`, `icrisat_wages.csv`, plus district boundaries `icrisat_districts.geojson`. Source: http://data.icrisat.org/dld/

**SHRUG** (Development Data Lab), district-level tables at 2011 boundaries, kept zipped under `shrug/`. Population Census PCA, village and town directories (1991, 2001, 2011), Economic Census (1990, 2005, 2013), SECC rural and SECC consumption, DMSP and VIIRS night lights, elevation, ruggedness, forest cover, PM2.5, RBI bank directory, and the shrid and district keys. Source: https://www.devdatalab.org/shrug

**CGWB** groundwater assessment 2011, district level (`cgwb_district_2011.csv`), used in NB4.

**Crosswalks built in the project:** `pc11_district_names.csv` and `pc11_to_icrisat_bridge.csv` map 2011 Census districts to ICRISAT parent districts. The bridge was fuzzy-matched and hand-corrected (see the Baran footnote in the paper).

Intermediate parquet files (`features.parquet`, `rainfall_shock.parquet`, `crops_clean.parquet` and so on) are regenerated by running the notebooks in order.
